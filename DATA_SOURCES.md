# Data sources and modifications

| Component | Source and terms | Use in this release |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solar observations | Manajit Sengupta and Afshin Andreas, *Oahu Solar Measurement Grid (1-Year Archive): 1-Second Solar Irradiance; Oahu, Hawaii*, NREL. [Dataset and attribution](https://data.openei.org/submissions/8121), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.7799/1052451), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | The processed PV inputs are an adaptation. Sensor readings are mapped to normalized site output, then sampled into causal histories, five-second targets and continuous windows. The study uses April, May and September 2010. The PV series are proxies for generation on the simulated feeder. |
| Distribution feeder and load profiles | [OEDI IEEE123 dataset](https://doi.org/10.25984/2228282), Alliance for Sustainable Energy, LLC. [Upstream BSD 3-Clause notice](licenses/OEDI-BSD-3-Clause.txt) | The feeder snapshot in `ieee123_direct_confirm_v1` contains the adapted phase-domain model and load series used in training and replay. One existing transformer interface and five added service areas host the six HPTs. |
| Transformer parameters | [IEEE PES test feeders](https://ewh.ieee.org/soc/pes/dsacom/testfeeders/testfeeders.pdf) and [Hayward et al., coordination of smart hybrid transformers](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSG.2024.3508252) | The experiment configuration records the transformer ratings, winding parameters, service connections and assumed converter fraction. |

Use the upstream attribution and license for distributed solar observations and derivatives, and retain the OEDI license notice with the feeder and profile data. The project's MIT software license applies to original code. The locked manifests include file hashes and source archive identifiers for provenance. The feeder snapshot is a Python pickle used by the original replay; use the checksummed copy from this release.

The archived CSVs contain window-level outcomes of simulated HPT actions, driven by measured irradiance and the feeder profiles. Times are elapsed controller times from the original hardware. Dates, selection rules and units are given in the [experiment report](hpt_project/runs/ieee123_expanded_cuda_v1/FINAL_RESULTS.md) and [trajectory manifest](hpt_project/runs/ieee123_expanded_trajectories_v1/manifest.csv).
